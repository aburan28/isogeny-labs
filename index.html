01 / WHAT WE DO

Deep cryptography.
Shipped systems.

A security level is a claim about how much work an attack takes. We take that claim seriously — analyzing it, attacking it, and then building against what the analysis says.

01Hardness analysis

Establish what a primitive actually costs to break, with measured attack work rather than folklore security levels.

  • Concrete cost models for discrete-log and isogeny problems
  • Index-calculus, descent, and generic-group bounds
  • Parameter selection tied to a stated attack budget
THE OUTCOME A defensible security level, with the analysis behind it.
02Isogeny & elliptic-curve systems

Design and implement schemes built on isogeny and elliptic-curve structure, where the mathematics is unforgiving.

  • SQIsign, CSIDH-family, and pairing-based constructions
  • Endomorphism-ring and torsion-point handling
  • Reference implementations with test vectors
THE OUTCOME Working code that matches the specification.
03Post-quantum migration

Move production systems onto standardized post-quantum primitives without breaking what already runs.

  • Cryptographic inventory and dependency mapping
  • Hybrid key establishment and crypto-agility design
  • Rollout gates, interoperability, and rollback planning
THE OUTCOME A migration your team can execute in stages.
04Protocol review

Audit the assumptions, proofs, and implementation details your security actually rests on.

  • Protocol and security-proof analysis
  • Key management, randomness, and side-channel review
  • Prioritized findings with remediation guidance
THE OUTCOME Clear findings, ranked by what matters.

02 / THE FOUNDATIONS

New standards.
Old assumptions
worth checking.

Post-quantum migration is more than an algorithm swap. Keys, certificates, protocols, libraries, and operational processes all have to hold together — and each new primitive rests on a hardness assumption that is younger than the one it replaces.

We work on both halves: the engineering that makes a transition survivable, and the analysis that tells you how much margin the underlying problem really has.

03 / HOW WE WORK

Stated assumptions.
Measured results.

Clear scope, close collaboration, and conclusions backed by something you can rerun. We work alongside your engineers, with a useful output at every stage.

  1. 01

    Model

    Write down the system, the adversary, and the assumptions. Agree on what would count as a break.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Attack the construction on paper and in code. Measure the cost of the best attack we can build.

  3. 03

    Build

    Implement against the analysis, with test vectors, benchmarks, and known-answer checks.

  4. 04

    Transfer

    Hand over the write-up, the code, and the reasoning so your team can carry it forward.
