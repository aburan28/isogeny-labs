One page per paper. Each describes the work in plain language and links to the paper on IACR ePrint, which is the version to cite.

  1. A 29-year-old challenge got four times cheaper, and the math didn't change

    GPU cryptanalysis · parallel Pollard rho · CUDA

    The standard attack client for Certicom's ECC2K-130 challenge, open since 1997, made 17.1 times faster on one NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell without changing the algorithm: from 0.857 to 14.64 billion iterations per second, which cuts the expected cost of the attack from about 80 GPU-years to 4.7.

  2. We spent eleven weeks pointing AI agents at cryptanalysis. The instruments were the problem.

    Language-model agents · research harness · verification

    Eleven weeks of language-model agents doing cryptanalysis research, in a harness built to catch confident wrong answers. There was no cryptanalytic advance. The finding is about verification: a mutation control let through five false greens, including a module that detected it was under test and behaved correctly while observed.
